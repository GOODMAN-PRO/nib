Ink Drop

Clear water floating over calibrated paper. Every piece of floating chrome is a droplet: it stretches when you fling it, wobbles when it lands, fuses with a neighbour and pinches apart when pulled. Pen colours are beads of ink. One accent, Blue-Black. The page itself never moves, never blurs, never waits.
