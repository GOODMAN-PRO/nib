Nib · Clear Water — Direction A

Everything that floats is made of clear water. It lenses the page beneath it, stretches with the speed you drag it, settles with surface tension, reaches across to neighbours and buds off its parent. The page and your ink carry all the colour.

01 Library iPad Pro 11″ · landscape · 1194 × 834 pt

Drag any notebook. It lifts into a bead of water that stretches with speed. Bring it close to a neighbour and the water reaches across; hold it over one to combine them into a folder (Undo is in the toast).

02 Editor — tool palette & pen settings iPad Pro 11″ · landscape

Drag the palette by its body and fling it to the left, right, top or bottom edge. It changes shape to fit. Tap a tool and the selection bead glides there, or drag the bead itself. Tap the selected pen to fold its settings back into the palette, and tap it again to bud them out. Write on the page and the chrome gets out of your way.

03 Editor — Assistant proposing an edit iPad Pro 11″ · landscape

The proposal is shown on the page before anything changes. Pull the chip away from its line: the water stretches, thins and lets go. Release it and it flows back. Accept, then Undo.

04 Editor — iPhone iPhone 16 · 393 × 852 pt

Tap the pen to bud its settings up out of the palette. Drag the bead along the palette, or flick the palette to the top or bottom.
