Nib · Locked design

Clear Water

Everything that floats in Nib is made of clear water. It lenses the page, stretches with the speed you drag it, settles with surface tension, reaches across to its neighbours, pinches apart when pulled and buds off its parent. The page and your ink carry all the colour. This page runs on the numbers in docs/DESIGN.md.

01 Library iPad Pro 11″ · landscape · 1194 × 834 pt

Drag a notebook. It lifts into a bead of water that stretches with speed and reaches toward its neighbours. Drop it on a folder tile (every tile grows a water film while you drag) or on a folder in the sidebar (the notebook condenses to half size and the folder grows a “+1”). Hold it over the middle of another notebook for a moment to make a folder. Every move has Undo.

02 Editor · tool palette and pen settings iPad Pro 11″ · landscape

Drag the palette by its body and fling it to any edge: it gathers into a bead and spreads into its new shape, and nothing ever draws outside it. Fling it to the bottom right and it fuses with the page counter. Tap tools and the bead glides as one drop; More buds the rest of the tools; press the selected tool and slide to scrub. Tap the selected pen to fold its settings in, and again to bud them out. Write on the page and the chrome steps back.

03 Editor · Assistant proposing an edit iPad Pro 11″ · landscape

The proposal is on the page before anything changes: numbered marks that match the rows, ghost ink in your own pen, a strike through the wrong formula. Untick a change and it leaves the page. The chip rests in the margin, clear of your ink. Pull it: an anchor appears on the change, the stem thins, pinches and leaves a small drop that flows home. The stray scribble has its own Delete button, because Accept never covers a deletion.

04 Editor · iPhone iPhone 16 · 393 × 852 pt

Tap the pen to bud its settings up out of the palette. Press the pen and slide along the palette to scrub the bead, or flick the palette to the top or bottom.

05 New notebook and templates iPad Pro 11″ · form sheet 720 × 640

A sheet is an opaque grouped surface. Its only water is the Create button. Pick a type, a cover and a paper.

06 Plugin manager iPad Pro 11″ · Settings › Plugins

Every permission is a plain sentence with its own switch. An update that asks for more shows exactly what changed before you accept it. Install from Gallery shows the consent sheet with the source and hash.

07 Settings iPad Pro 11″ · form sheet

Grouped lists stay native. The switch thumb is the only liquid here: it squashes as it moves. Liquid: Full, Calm or Off changes every frame on this page.
