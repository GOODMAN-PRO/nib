## Rendering evidence and unresolved diagnosis

The audit group is named `V2:test13DocumentSearch#0`, but its evidence is `G:/Projects/Nib-ci-logs/scratch-visual2/n_test14AssistantDarkPortrait.png`. That image shows blank paper and an empty palette body while the options bar and assistant are present. The current caller is `CaptureUITests.capture14Assistant`, shared by all four `test14Assistant*` methods. `capture13DocumentSearch` is a separate search scenario.

The source establishes a normal rendering path after layout, not a guarantee that the historical failure eventually recovered:

1. `FeatCanvas/CanvasViewController.swift:viewDidLayoutSubviews` detects viewport/inset changes and calls `relayout`; visible-page/tile updates run through `DocumentScrollView`. `FeatCanvas/PageTileView.swift:request(_:slot:page:)` and `requestPreviewIfNeeded(_:)` asynchronously call the tile source. `FeatCanvas/CanvasHostImpl.swift:renderTile` awaits `services.renderer.render`.
2. On a successful, still-current result, both tile and preview paths set their layer contents and call `PageTileView.contentLanded()`. That sets `hasShownContent` and brings `contentView.alpha` to 1 (120 ms fade on iOS 18+, immediate on iOS 17). This explains why item counts/accessibility records are insufficient readiness evidence. Failure/unavailable/cancellation paths do not establish eventual successful rendering.
3. `NibDesign/Components/Palette.swift:follow(_:model:)` either changes layout directly, starts a field reshape, or schedules a reduced-motion cross-fade back to `fade = 1`. `reshaped(_:)` adopts the target layout at `.spreading`. `NibDesign/Liquid/DropletField.swift` returns a settled spreading shape to `.idle` and restores `contentAlpha = 1`; its presentation carries that as `contentOpacity`. These are successful transition paths, not evidence that the failed capture reached them.

**The underlying blank-rendering defect remains unresolved.** There is no simulator or later same-session screenshot here that proves timing, and a static success path cannot rule out a persistent renderer or palette presentation failure. The former unconditional timing diagnosis in the capture comment has therefore been removed.
